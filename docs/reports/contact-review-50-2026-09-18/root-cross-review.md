# Independent checks during the 50-target review

- **ITGB3/2Q6W:** live RCSB title and deposited content identify an integrin-derived peptide presented by HLA-DR52a. HLA-DRA/DRB3 contacts do not establish intact integrin–HLA receptor interactions. Preserve source contacts and target-fragment context. https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2Q6W
- **CALCRL and RAMP1/5V6Y:** primary study describes AM(37–52) S45W/K46L/Q50W/Y52F antagonist, not PAMP-20. RCSB identifies an MBP–RAMP1–CLR fusion and a 17-residue deposited ADM construct; retain terminal-chemistry/construct detail. https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5V6Y ; https://doi.org/10.1124/mol.117.110916
- **ITGB6 (and prior ITGAV)/5FFO:** main RCSB mutation flag says No, but primary methods explicitly report C4S, R249A, N107Q and N147Q in pro-TGF-beta1. Reconcile deposited sequence with paper numbering and retain discrepancies. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5586147/ ; https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5FFO
- **TNF/3L9J:** the named CLEC3B source represents an engineered TNF antagonist scaffold; a parent accession alone is insufficient evidence of a native ligand. https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3L9J
- **MR1/7RNO:** integrated docking/NMR/restrained-MD model, with hybrid MR1 and bovine B2M. Preserve model provenance and intracellular peptide-loading context; do not describe modeled contacts as crystal observations. https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7RNO

These flags were sent to the relevant reviewers before final rule delivery. Final accepted rules and outcomes are recorded after independent cross-review.
