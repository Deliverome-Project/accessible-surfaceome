**Supplementary Figure 1. Supplying protein-coding gene identifiers, but not live retrieval tools, drives within-tier accuracy gains in language-model triage on the 147-protein benchmark.** Bar height is the mean across replicates of overall verdict accuracy, scoring a call correct when a contextually-surface protein is labeled surface under a soft-credit rule. Four Claude tiers (Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 4.6, Sonnet 5, Opus 4.8) are each compared under up to four prompt-context conditions: gene symbol alone (naive); symbol plus a cached gene summary and stable HGNC/UniProt identifiers (+IDs); identifier context plus an on-demand web-search tool; and identifier context plus on-demand PubMed retrieval. Models were run at three replicates per protein per condition (overlaid points; error bars SEM). Sonnet 4.6 with identifiers is the production triage configuration.
