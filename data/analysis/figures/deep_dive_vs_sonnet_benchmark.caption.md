The deep-dive bar is stacked by the tier it assigned to the correctly-classified genes — canonical / likely / low (green shades) and uncertain / no (grey), the same five-tier palette as Figure 5a — so its confidence composition is visible: ground-truth-yes genes are called canonical + likely, contextual genes likely + low, and the correct not-surface calls land in uncertain / no. Sonnet+NCBI accuracy is the mean of three mainbench replicates (one dot per replicate; the error bar is the SEM across replicates); the deep dive runs once per gene, so its bar carries no replicate spread. The deep-dive verdict counts as surface when its tier is canonical, likely, or low (the PR #130 canonical gate); Sonnet and the ground truth count as surface when yes or contextual. Deep dive and Sonnet are near-identical overall (98% vs 97%), and Sonnet's SEM overlaps the deep-dive bar in every bucket, so the difference is not significant at this n.
