Release: `contacts-ba6680878403e6980464`. Eight stable target accessions reviewed against all 247 supplied raw observations and named overviews. Proposed changes only; no observations, source checkout, database, or deployed assets were modified.

The rules preserve raw construct labels and contacts. ## Uncertain findings and required scoped follow-up

1. **CD47/SIRPA construct mixing:** P78324 groups native SIRPA (2JJS/2JJT/4CMM) with engineered IMM01 SIRPa.D1(N80A) in [7YGG](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7YGG). Proposed all-PDB category remains unclassified, with an explicit mixed-construct label. A later PDB-scoped rule should separate 7YGG. On the reciprocal SIRPA target, the CD47 partner remains native but target-construct provenance must note engineered SIRPA.
2. **SIRPA AB21 versus Fab 25:** [7KPG](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7KPG) calls the Fab 25; [the paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13045-020-00989-w) distinguishes AB21 from humanized hAB21 and describes the crystallized Fab as hAB21. IEDB uses AB21. Keep separate pending deposited-sequence reconciliation.
3. **MSLN 3F2 discrepancy:** [8CXC](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8CXC) deposits a 3F2 Fab and an amatuximab scFv, but [the primary paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/drug-discovery/articles/10.3389/fddsv.2023.1216516/full) describes 1A12 and contains an inconsistent accession sentence. 3F2 stays separate/unclassified. By contrast, the 8CYH A12 Fab and 8CZ8 1A12 scFv are supported as 1A12 aliases. Neither is anetumab.
4. **BCMA variants:** J22.9-xi, J22.9-H, J22.9-FNY, J22.9-ISY remain four distinct constructs. [8QYB](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8QYB) describes ISY as fully humanized and CDR optimized; the cached exact VH/VL Pamlectabart match is retained as an arm link, not proof of an ADC complex. Pamlectabart tismanitin is a separate ADC, and no payload is modeled. BCMA target in 8QYB has four listed mutations.
5. **MS4A1 source identity conflict:** [3BKY](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3BKY) is C2H7, not a deposited ibritumomab construct, despite the cached exact-VH/VL drug association. Do not merge these names; exclude the catalogue-only drug from overview. [3PP4](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3PP4) is GA101 Fab, not intact glofitamab. The IEDB aggregate GA101/B-Ly1/H299 label mixes parent and derivative names and no-PDB records; leave unclassified pending per-assay inspection.
6. **DR5 Fc engineering:** cached 6T3J chain pairs A/B and F/G match Benufutamab; C/D and H/I match Tilogotamab. [PDB entities](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6T3J) name these IgG1-hDR5-01 and -05. Keep the two observed Fabs separate; full Fc-engineered drugs and the combination are not crystallized.
7. **CD47 bispecifics:** [7XJF](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7XJF) contains 6MW3211 CD47-binding Fab; [8RP8](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8RP8) contains K2 Fab. Safimestomig and Zeripatamig source links remain separate therapeutic catalogue observations, excluded from the direct-partner overview. The latter cache uses Thera_100pct_structure_chain, not exact_VH_VL.
8. **HER3 bispecific:** [5O4O](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5O4O) and 5O7P are MF3178 Fab complexes; the zenocutuzumab match does not turn them into intact HER2/HER3 bispecific structures. Keep MF3178 observed and zenocutuzumab catalogue-linked.

## Additional raw-observation findings (not promoted by these rules)

- ERBB3 P0AEX9 is the bacterial maltose-binding portion of a HER2 fusion entity, as documented by [7MN8](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7MN8). It must not become a new endogenous HER3 ligand; overlapping chain contacts may be attributed to multiple accessions by the source. S6B291 at 5CUS needs antibody-chain identity resolution before naming; it is not evidence for a new native ligand.
- TNFRSF17 Q9D777 at [1XU2](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1XU2) is **mouse APRIL** complexed with human BCMA. A future named row must retain the species distinction; do not call it human APRIL.
- MSLN anonymous SAbDab2 identifier `sabdab2_H05R3L04BX` at [9P4C](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9P4C) can be investigated/promoted as RO4 Fab with a C-terminal peptide. It is not another MORAb-009 observation.
- DR5 Q6RJQ3 at [4I9X](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4I9X) is viral HCMV UL141, a real exogenous viral binding partner, not endogenous TRAIL. Current category enum lacks viral/exogenous; retain unclassified rather than call it therapeutic or endogenous.
- Generic immunoglobulin accessions (MS4A1 P01834/P01857; DR5 P01861/V9HW34) and anonymous SAbDab2 records remain raw. Do not promote them to independent native partners or infer clone equivalence solely from matching footprints.

## Matcher and preservation checks

All 75 proposed rules use literal input labels and have at least one matching raw or summary record. No supplied named EC summary is unmatched; no raw or named-summary observation matches conflicting proposed rules. Numeric IEDB IDs are intentionally not used as match names: `209111` occurs for both J22.9-FNY and J22.9-ISY; `690` occurs for J22.9-H and J22.9-xi. Matching uses their distinct partner_label values. The matcher strips a trailing Fab/Fv/VHH and parenthetical qualifier, but does not strip scFv, diabody, or Nanobody; these exact labels are enumerated. Exclusion is sticky in the production matcher, so do not append broad overlapping exclusions after these rules.
