How to interpret this pilot

Coverage counts unique HGNC genes using canonical identifiers from public D1. The receptor subset is the existing classical_surface_receptor triage category; it does not include every receptor class. Exact existing reagents require a clone, catalog number or RRID. Literature-extracted annotations have not been independently reaudited. “Validated live-cell” additionally requires moderate/strong recorded validation beyond vendor claims.

IUPHAR Kd/Ki measurements are distinguished from IC50/EC50 potency. BioGRID affinity values retain their original text because the table does not always specify Kd versus another endpoint. BioGRID “Surface Display” may mean yeast or phage display and does not establish binding on mammalian cells. Explicit IUPHAR complex-component associations are excluded from headline single-protein coverage.
