**Figure 4. Database and triage calls select 5,332 genes for the deep dive, which runs in three sequential stages, producing one validated record.** (a) Every protein-coding gene is assessed by the five reference databases and, in parallel, by the two-stage triage agent. The union of the two inputs is trimmed of proteins represented by a single database that the triage agent also called high-confidence non-surface with localization to clear non-surface compartments (noted in the figure), leaving 5,332 genes for the deep dive. (b) The per-gene deep-dive pipeline runs in three sequential stages, producing one validated record. Stage 1, evidence retrieval: public databases build a candidate corpus, a per-paper Haiku 4.5 abstract triage selects papers worth fetching, open-access full text is recovered through a tiered fallback, and a Haiku 4.5 trim and Sonnet 4.6 selector yield per-protein evidence sets. Stage 2, block builders (Sonnet 4.6): builders draft the record's surface-evidence, surface-biology, and risk sections. Stage 3, synthesis (Sonnet 4.6): the synthesizer reads every section draft plus the Stage 1 evidence set, deterministic features, and the triage verdict, then writes the executive summary, structured filters, and confidence rationale.
