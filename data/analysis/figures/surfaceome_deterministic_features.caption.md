**Across the deep-dived surfaceome, the twelve precomputed deterministic features distribute as expected for bona-fide surface proteins, and the pattern already tracks the real deep-dive confidence tier.** Each deep-dived gene is faceted by its real deep-dive surface-accessibility tier, using the top two tiers as-is and pooling only the two weakest: canonical (the high-confidence surface tier), likely, low (low/moderate accessibility on weak evidence), and uncertain / no (the ambiguous-to-negative tiers combined). A fifth facet — the full Sonnet dual-triage pool (every gene the genome-wide triage called yes or contextual, ~4,236 with topology) — is shown alongside the four deep-dive tiers for comparison; its deterministic features are drawn from the genome-wide tables rather than the deep-dive records, since most of these genes are not deep-dived. Genes that are Sonnet-positive but not yet deep-dived carry a pending tier and are excluded from the four deep-dive-tier facets, since they have no deep-dive verdict to compare. The 4×3 grid compares one deterministic feature per panel: DeepTMHMM transmembrane-helix count, protein length, and extracellular-domain (ECD) length (violins), and the fraction carrying a signal peptide, an extracellular N-terminus, an extracellular C-terminus, a 1:1 mouse ortholog, a 1:1 cyno ortholog (Ensembl Compara), a predicted homo-oligomer state (Schweke 2024), an alternative isoform with a topology differing from canonical, at least one predicted extracellular surface-binding site (its anchor residue on the outside/extracellular face of the topology, excluding intracellular and membrane-buried sites), and a concerning paralog whose extracellular domain is ≥40% identical (a potential antibody cross-reactivity risk) (fraction bars — a violin of a 0/1 value is meaningless). The extracellular surface-binding-site fraction rises steeply with confidence (canonical 47% → uncertain/no 3%), as expected for genuine surface targets. Topology and every deterministic feature shown here are now sourced from the deep-dive RECORDS (full coverage per deep-dived gene), fixing the prior DeepTMHMM-M1-only coverage bias where the low, uncertain, and no tiers were 70–94% missing. Each value is a tool-derived entry on the immutable deterministic_features record block, reproducible from the bundled per-gene TSV. Computed over the full deep-dive cohort (5,130 genes); the `canonical` tier is the frontend catalog-preset predicate (`passesCanonical`).
