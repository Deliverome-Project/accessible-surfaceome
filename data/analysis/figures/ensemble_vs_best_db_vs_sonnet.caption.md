**Supplementary Figure 5. Ensembling the five gold-standard surface databases does not close the accuracy gap to the Sonnet 4.6 triage agent.** Overall accuracy on the 147 protein-coding gene SurfaceBench (soft-credit rule: a contextually-surface protein counts correct when called surface) is shown for seven predictors: the production triage agent (Sonnet 4.6 with gene identifiers; mean of three replicates), the single best-performing database, and five ensemble predictors requiring at least one (the union), two, three, four, or all five databases to agree. Each database (UniProt, GO cellular component, HPA, SURFY, CSPA) is applied under its SurfaceBench-calibrated inclusion rule. Accuracy peaks when two databases must agree (83.7%) and falls on either side: combining the databases suppresses surface calls faster than it corrects them and never recovers the proteins they jointly miss.
