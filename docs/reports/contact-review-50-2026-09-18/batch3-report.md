## Remaining limitations

- No-PDB DA5/DA10/DA330 EPOR identities are not collapsed into numbered diabodies without direct equivalence evidence.
- Receptor-subunit groups are assembly-role summaries. Full construct, allele, splice and chemical reconciliation is not claimed.
- Presented foreign peptides, non-native proteins and unresolved fragment chemistry remain unclassified where the current category enum is insufficient.
- Generic BioLiP records need chain recovery when several candidate entities exist. Shared PDB or footprint is not normally enough to assign a clone.
- Primary metadata, source IDs and residue sets remain intact; all proposed changes are additive annotations, not contact recomputation or evidence deletion.
