**Figure 1. Five surface protein databases agree on only 188 proteins.** UpSet plot over five databases with surface protein annotations; each bar counts proteins found in that database or combination of databases and no others, grouped by how many databases list the protein, with total set sizes at left. Each set is the union of UniProt accessions labeled as surface proteins under the source-specific rule (UniProt: strict surface terms or extracellular topology; GO CC: non-inferred from electronic annotation (IEA) to a surface-relevant term; HPA: Enhanced/Supported/Approved reliability for Plasma membrane or Cell Junctions; SURFY: published binary classifier output; CSPA: high-confidence or putative, additional details in Methods).
