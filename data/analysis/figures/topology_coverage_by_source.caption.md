**Supplementary Figure 9. Each surface source is enriched for a distinct set of membrane-topology architectures, but that enrichment and its genome-wide coverage are separate quantities.** Bubble matrix of inclusion sources (rows) × topology features (columns) over the candidate-surfaceome universe: dot area is within-source enrichment (fraction of that source's own calls carrying the feature) and dot color is coverage (fraction of the whole surface universe those proteins represent). Cell-Surface-Capture CSPA is strongly glycosylation-enriched (~96% of its own calls) yet contributes only a small slice of the universe (~14%), reflecting its N-glycocapture chemistry and limitations of experimental detection on cell lines; SURFY and UniProt show the same glycosylation enrichment at larger scale, while the zero-database Sonnet rescues differ by how the triage agent called them: those called from the gene summary alone (no literature; 794) are mostly predicted secreted (55%), and 41% are glycosylated, whereas those rescued only once PubMed literature was added (314) most often lack both a transmembrane segment and a signal peptide (49%), proteins whose surface localization is evident only from the literature.
