**Two-panel summary of how the deep-dive agent sorts the surface cohort, and the cross-cutting cell-type and cell-state facets that overlay the surface tiers.** (a) The deep-dived cohort placed on a five-tier confidence spectrum: `canonical` (the strict gold-standard surface tier), `likely` (the broader passes-likely surface set), then the below-likely genes split by the deep-dive's tentative call — `low` (low/moderate accessibility but weak evidence — maybe surface), `no` (leaned not-surface), and `uncertain` (ambiguous). `canonical` and `likely` are exactly the frontend catalog-preset predicates (`passesCanonical` / `passesLikely`); the low/no/uncertain split of the negatives is a figure-only refinement (the presets don't cover the negatives). (b) The two cross-cutting surface facets — cell-type restricted and cell-state induced — counted by the deep dive's surface-call **reason** and broken out across the three surface-bearing tiers (canonical / likely / low). Counting by reason rather than the evidence-gated facet keeps the weak-evidence surface calls that land in `low`, which the gated facet drops. Each tier bar stacks three categories: cell-type restricted (`tissue_restricted_surface`), induced-oncogenic (`cell_state_induced` / `lysosomal_exocytosis` with an oncogenic trigger), and induced-other (any other inducing trigger). The facets cut across confidence rather than gating it: canonical carries 221 induced-oncogenic + 40 induced-other calls and, by construction, no cell-type restricted (the canonical gate excludes `tissue_restricted_surface`); likely adds 852 cell-type restricted, 125 induced-oncogenic, 68 induced-other; low adds 471 cell-type restricted, 88 induced-oncogenic, 90 induced-other. Totals across the three tiers: 1,323 cell-type restricted and 632 cell-state induced (434 oncogenic, 198 other). Computed over the full deep-dive cohort (5,332 genes); `canonical` and `likely` are the frontend catalog-preset predicates, giving 1,783 canonical / 1,252 likely / 1,014 low / 1,228 no / 55 uncertain.
