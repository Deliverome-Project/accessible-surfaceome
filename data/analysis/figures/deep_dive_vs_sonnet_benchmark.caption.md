**On the SurfaceBench genes deep-dived so far, the evidence-anchored deep dive edges out the Sonnet+NCBI triage's soft-credit accuracy, and its confidence tier tracks ground-truth difficulty.** For all 113 SurfaceBench benchmark genes deep-dived so far (the intersection with the 147-gene benchmark; no records are excluded — including the one holistic-`conflicting` gene, RPN1, whose A1 experimental ledger and A2 biological-context picture disagree — so both predictors are scored on the full common set), each protein's deep-dive surface call and the Sonnet+NCBI triage call are scored against the curated ground-truth verdict under the soft-credit rule — a contextually-surface protein counts correct when called surface. (a) Overall soft-credit accuracy. (b) Accuracy split by ground-truth bucket (yes / contextual / no). The deep-dive bar is stacked by the tier it assigned to the correctly-classified genes — canonical / likely / low (green shades) and uncertain / no (grey), the same five-tier palette as Figure 5a — so its confidence composition is visible: ground-truth-yes genes are called canonical + likely, contextual genes likely + low, and the correct not-surface calls land in uncertain / no. Sonnet+NCBI accuracy is the mean of three mainbench replicates (one dot per replicate; the error bar is the SEM across replicates); the deep dive runs once per gene, so its bar carries no replicate spread. The deep-dive verdict counts as surface when its tier is canonical, likely, or low (the PR #130 canonical gate); Sonnet and the ground truth count as surface when yes or contextual. Deep dive and Sonnet are near-identical overall (97% vs 97% — the Sonnet bar is the mean of its three per-replicate accuracies; on a single collapsed-verdict basis the deep dive is 97.3% to Sonnet's 95.6%), and Sonnet's SEM overlaps the deep-dive bar in every bucket, so the difference is not significant at this n. The deep dive is perfect on the 68 ground-truth-yes genes (100%) and makes three errors: one false negative in the 31-gene contextual bucket (PMEL, a `dual_localization_borderline` melanosomal antigen the deep dive's literature search under-recalled) and two false positives in the 14-gene `no` bucket (ATG9A, a state-gated endomembrane scramblase; and RPN1, the holistic-`conflicting` ER-resident whose surfaceome-MS hit the deep dive could not fully dismiss).
