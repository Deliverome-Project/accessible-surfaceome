**Supplementary Figure 1. Supplying protein-coding gene identifiers, but not web search or pre-fetched PubMed abstracts, drives within-tier accuracy gains in language-model triage on the 147-protein benchmark.** Bars show mean soft-credit verdict accuracy across replicates. Eight Claude models (Haiku 4.5 and 5.5; Sonnet 4.6, 5 and 5.5; Opus 4.8, 5 and 5.5) are compared under up to four conditions: gene symbol alone (naive); symbol plus a cached gene summary and stable HGNC/UniProt identifiers (+IDs); identifier context plus an on-demand web-search tool; and identifier context plus PubMed abstract excerpts retrieved in advance and appended to the prompt. Opus 5.5 was also run with identifiers at low reasoning effort. Three replicates per protein per condition (points; error bars SEM). Refused calls (Opus 5.5 only) are excluded from accuracy, and the share refused is printed on the bar. Color indicates model generation, and lavender marks the two configurations the production pipeline uses. Sonnet 4.6 with identifiers is the production triage configuration.
