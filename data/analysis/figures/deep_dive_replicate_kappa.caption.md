**Deep-dive records are reproducible, and most run-to-run differences come from which papers are retrieved.** We re-analyzed 50 randomly selected deep-dive proteins in two ways and compared each of the 24 LLM-derived fields, plus the surface call and confidence tier, with the published record. In the full re-run (maroon, solid), we repeated the entire pipeline from literature discovery onward with identical prompts. In the fixed-evidence replay (lavender, dotted), we reused each record's selected evidence and re-ran only the section builders and synthesizer. Points show Cohen's κ versus the published record, with 95% intervals from 5,000 bootstrap resamples of proteins. Ordered fields use linearly weighted κ, and all others use unweighted κ. State dependence and co-receptor dependency exclude unclear and unknown calls (n shown). Green shading marks moderate (0.60–0.79), strong (0.80–0.90) and almost perfect (>0.90) agreement (McHugh, 2012). Induction trigger is scored as a single dominant trigger, although several can apply to one protein. Shed form is rare (4 of 50 proteins), which lowers κ despite 92% raw agreement.
